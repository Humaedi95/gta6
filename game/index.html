Open-World Crime · Solmar Bay

Sun-soaked streets, fast cars and faster money. Take five jobs from fixer Mara Voss, run them across Solmar Bay, steal what you need, and stay one step ahead of the Solmar Bay Police Department.

An original game. No real city, person or brand is depicted. Keyboard + mouse.

HOW TO PLAY

WASD / Arrows — walk & drive   Shift — sprint   E — steal / enter / exit car

Mouse — aim   Click — shoot   F — punch   Space — handbrake

P — pause   M — mute   H — toggle help

Follow the yellow marker and the arrow at the edge of the screen to reach each job. Stealing cars, hurting people and shooting raise your wanted level — the police will chase you. Lose them, or drive through the Lube & Tune garage to clear your name for $250.

Earn cash from jobs, taxi fares and the 20 golden shells hidden around the bay. Buy ammo at Ironclad Arms. Your progress saves automatically.
